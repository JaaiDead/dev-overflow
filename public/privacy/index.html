Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This is a static portfolio site. It has no backend server, user accounts, analytics, tracking scripts, or cookies. Nothing you do here is collected or stored on infrastructure operated by this site.

Local storage

The selected theme and portfolio profile are saved in your browser localStorage so they persist between visits. This information stays on your device.

Third-party services

The site fetches public project data from the Modrinth API and links to GitHub, Modrinth, CurseForge, Discord, and Gmail. Those services have their own privacy policies and handle visits outside this site.

Contact

Email links open a prefilled Gmail compose window. No message is sent until you review and send it yourself.
